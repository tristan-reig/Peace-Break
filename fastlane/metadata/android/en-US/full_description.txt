Peace Break is a brick breaker game with a CRT arcade look.

Bounce the ball off your paddle to clear ten levels of increasing
difficulty. Each level has its own time limit: the faster you clear it,
the higher your score. Keep your lives to earn an extra bonus.

Features:

* Ten hand-crafted levels
* Power-ups and power-downs to catch mid-game
* Coins to spend on paddle and ball skins
* Online leaderboard
* Progress synced across devices via your account

An account is required to play, as scores and progress are stored
online.
